LOGOS
=====

Drop client, advisor, university, and association logos here, then wire them
into the logo row on index.html (inside <div class="logo-row">).

Replace an empty slot:

  <div class="logo-slot">Client logo<br>slot 1</div>

with:

  <div class="logo-slot filled"><img src="assets/logos/acme.png" alt="Acme Industrial"></div>

Specs
-----
· PNG or SVG with a transparent background
· Roughly 40px tall at display size; supply at 2x (about 80-120px tall)
· Grayscale or single-color versions read best against the cream background
· Keep every logo to a similar visual weight so the row looks even

Rules
-----
· Publish a client logo ONLY where that client has agreed in writing.
· Use "As advised by ..." wording only for advisors who have agreed to be
  named. Advisor slots also exist on team.html (#advisors).
· If a slot has no real logo, delete the slot rather than leaving the dashed
  placeholder on the live site.

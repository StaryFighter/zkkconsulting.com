Referral Program

Know a business that could use us?

Introduce us. When your referral signs and fully pays for their first engagement, you receive 15% off your next consulting package.

Make an introduction See what to refer them to
How It Works

Three steps.

01
Introduce us

Email us with the introduction, or have them mention your name when they reach out. Either works, as long as we can connect the referral to you before their engagement is signed.

02
They engage us

We scope the work with them the same way we would with anyone. Your reward activates once their engagement is signed and paid in full.

03
You save 15%

We issue your reward, and you apply it to your next package or item with us. No cap on how many businesses you introduce.

Program Terms

The rules, plainly.

  • The 15% reward applies to one future package or item, not to an entire multi-item order.
  • The reward activates only after the referred client's initial and final invoices are paid in full.
  • The reward expires 12 months after it is issued.
  • Bundles receive a maximum referral discount of 10%, in place of the 15%.
  • Referral rewards are not transferable, cannot be exchanged for cash, and cannot be combined with another referral reward on the same item.
  • We need to be able to attribute the referral to you before the referred client's Statement of Work is signed.
  • ZKK Consulting LLC may modify or end the program at any time. Rewards already issued remain valid through their expiration date.

Questions about a specific situation? Email us and we will tell you how it would work before you make the introduction.

Make the Introduction

Send them our way.

A one-line email introduction is plenty. We take it from there, and we will keep you posted on whether it turns into an engagement.

What happens next
  1. Within 1 business day: we reply confirming fit and which package or custom scope matches.
  2. Scoping call: 30 minutes on your device, your market, and the question to answer.
  3. Kickoff: we send a Statement of Work to sign, then a Stripe invoice for the 50% deposit. Your timeline starts once both are in.
  4. On completion: the deliverable lands, and we invoice the remaining 50% separately.
How payment works

There is nothing to pay from this page. Once we've had our scoping call, we send a Statement of Work confirming scope, deliverable, timeline, and price. After you sign it, we email you a Stripe invoice for the 50% deposit, payable by card. Your timeline starts once the signed SOW and the deposit are both in, along with any files or information we identified before or during the call that we need to begin. The remaining 50% is invoiced separately on completion, when the finished deliverable is in your hands.

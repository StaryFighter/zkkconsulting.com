Standalone Service

Patent Landscape Analysis

Know what is already patented in your space before you commit engineering time, capital, or a market position. Available on its own or bundled with market research.

What the engagement covers

  • Specialized patent research across the technology area and competitive field you define with us
  • Review and comparison of up to 15 identified patents, read for what they actually claim and cover
  • A summary and comparison matrix that puts the patents side by side on the dimensions that matter to your product
  • Functional overlap analysis, showing where existing patents sit close to what you are building
  • Competitive considerations, including who is filing, where they are concentrating, and what that suggests about their direction
  • Potential business risks surfaced as considerations to discuss with counsel, not as legal conclusions
  • Written report with the full patent list, sources, and the search approach documented

Important: This is strategic business and market research only. ZKK Consulting is not providing legal advice, a legal patentability opinion, or a formal Freedom-to-Operate opinion, and does not determine infringement or the legal validity of any patent. Clients should consult qualified patent counsel for legal conclusions and determinations.

How we measure it

  • Pricing is based on the original document you send us, not the length of the reformatted version. A redesign that grows the page count does not raise your price.
  • We look at word count alongside page count so that a document with very small type or dense pages is priced the same way a normal one would be.
  • Unusually dense or complex files, or scope information that turns out to be inaccurate, may need an adjusted quote. We tell you before we start, not after.

Engagement Terms

How the work is structured

Payment terms

Fixed price per engagement with ZKK Consulting LLC. After we scope the work we send a Statement of Work to sign, then a Stripe invoice for the 50% deposit. Work begins once the signed SOW and the deposit are both in. The remaining 50% is invoiced on completion. Packages, standalone services, bundles, and custom scopes all work the same way.

Timelines

Timelines are established individually with each client and written into the Statement of Work. Depth of research, add-ons, how quickly we receive your materials, and your own deadlines all affect the schedule. If you are working toward a hard date, raise it early and we will tell you what is realistic.

One round of revisions

Every engagement includes one round of revisions. You have 5 business days after delivery to send one consolidated set of changes, and we refine the deliverable so it matches what we scoped.

Confidential by default

Your business and data stay private and are never reused, resold, or shared. We are glad to sign a mutual NDA before you share anything proprietary. Where an engagement warrants it, a limited non-solicitation or exclusivity term can be discussed and set out in the Statement of Work.

What you receive

Written analysis with sources and assumptions documented throughout, plus any models, matrices, or visuals in the scope. Models are delivered as editable files, not locked exports, so your team can keep using them after the engagement closes.

Scope of our work

We provide research and analysis. We present sources, assumptions, findings, estimates, and possible considerations. We do not provide legal, regulatory, medical, tax, accounting, or investment advice, and our deliverables are not a substitute for it.

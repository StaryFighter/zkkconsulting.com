Experience

Hands-on research for technical products.

Before ZKK Consulting, Zolomon spent nearly a year as Business Development Associate at SageSpectra, an early-stage biomedical device startup seeking seed-stage funding while running animal testing on its device. We work the same way for every client. We start from your specific goals, combine the data you provide with rigorous independent research from legitimate sources, and deliver finished, decision-ready documents on a schedule, both quick weekly reports and longer multi-week projects.

That is exactly the work in our Market Brief, Market Report, and Strategy Package, and in the custom research we scope outside them.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Do you sign NDAs?
Yes. We're glad to sign a mutual NDA before you share anything proprietary. Where an engagement warrants it, a limited non-solicitation or exclusivity term scoped to that project can be discussed and, if agreed, set out in the Statement of Work. Confidentiality is the default on every engagement. Your product, data, and findings stay private.
Who am I contracting with?
All engagements are with ZKK Consulting LLC, a Texas limited liability company co-founded by Zolomon Kaliser and Jad Haddad. The Master Services Agreement, Statement of Work, mutual NDA, and every invoice are issued in the name of ZKK Consulting LLC.
Which of you will I work with?
It depends on the work. Zolomon leads medtech, medical device, and financial-modeling engagements. Jad leads electronics, embedded systems, and broader technology-market research. Many projects touch both, in which case you'll work with both of us. We'll tell you who is running your project on the scoping call, before anything is signed.
Do you only work in medtech?
No. Medtech is where our deepest specialization sits, and it's where the sample deliverables on this site come from. But our research and analysis methods apply across engineering-driven and technical industries: electronics, embedded and connected products, industrial hardware, and adjacent markets. If you're unsure whether your field is a fit, email us and describe it. We'll give you a straight answer, including if the answer is no.
How does payment work?
Each engagement is a fixed price split into two parts. We start with a quick intake and scoping call to confirm fit and scope, and we then send a Statement of Work to sign. Once the SOW is signed, we email you a Stripe invoice for the 50% deposit. Work begins and your timeline starts once the signed SOW and the deposit are both in. The remaining 50% is invoiced separately on completion, when the finished deliverable is in your hands. This applies to the three packages and to custom scopes alike.
Do you offer revisions?
One round of revisions is included with every engagement. You have 5 business days after delivery to send one consolidated set of changes, and we'll refine the deliverable so it matches what we scoped. The walkthrough is scheduled separately, at a time that works for everyone.
How long does it take?
Timelines run to delivery: Market Brief in 5 business days, Market Report in 7, and the Strategy Package in 14. Custom scopes get their own timeline, quoted with the scope. The clock starts once the SOW is signed, the deposit is received, and your materials are in hand, whichever comes last. On a tighter deadline? Mention it when you email and we'll tell you what's possible.
What if my project doesn't fit a package?
Then email us and describe it. That is the point of the services list above. The three packages cover the most common requests, but a large share of our work is scoped from scratch. We'll work through it on the scoping call and send a custom fixed-price quote based on the scope we define together, on the same fixed-price basis the packages are built on. The packages are a starting point, not a ceiling.
Do you give recommendations or advice?
We provide research and analysis. We report what the sources show, document every assumption, and walk you through what the findings mean, so you can make the call with better information. We do not provide legal, regulatory, medical, tax, accounting, or investment advice, and nothing we deliver should be treated as a substitute for a licensed professional in those fields. Every business decision remains yours.
How is the research sourced?
Every assumption and figure is documented and cited, so you and your investors can trace each number back to its source. The goal is research you can defend in a board meeting, not a black box.

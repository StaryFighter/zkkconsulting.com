Specialized Expertise

Where our bench runs deepest

ZKK researches across industries. In a few areas we have training and hands-on experience that most research firms our size do not: medical devices and FDA-related research, electronics and FCC-related research, and engineering-led product development. These are premium and custom capabilities rather than standard package inclusions.

Book a Consultation See how these are priced

What this is, and what it is not. ZKK Consulting provides research, analysis, and technical consulting support. We research and explain what regulations, standards, databases, and published sources say, and we help organize the documentation and planning around them. We do not provide legal advice or legal opinions, we do not make regulatory or legal determinations, and we do not represent clients in regulatory, enforcement, or legal proceedings. Where a legal determination or representation is required, qualified counsel should be retained.

Medical Devices

FDA and medical device research

Regulatory and technical research for device companies, built on the public regulations, guidance documents, and FDA databases, and on graduate biomedical engineering training.

Pathway & classification research

  • Device classification research using FDA regulations and databases
  • Predicate device research and comparison
  • 510(k), De Novo, and pathway option research
  • Research on typical testing and documentation requirements
  • Q-Submission meeting preparation and background research
  • Project and milestone planning around the research

Quality & documentation support

  • Design controls and QMS research
  • Documentation organization and structure
  • Design History File organization support
  • Risk management and V&V documentation support
  • Labeling research and technical file organization
  • Literature and published evidence reviews

Testing & commercial landscape

  • Biocompatibility, EMC, and electrical safety testing research
  • Testing coordination and accredited laboratory research
  • Standards landscape research for a given device type
  • Reimbursement, coding, and coverage landscape research
  • Competitive and market research for device categories
  • Investor-facing market math for device companies

Wording matters here. We research and explain what the regulations, guidance, and databases say, and we provide technical and regulatory research support. We do not issue regulatory opinions, determine classification or clearance outcomes on the FDA's behalf, provide legal advice, or act as your regulatory agent or legal representative. Reimbursement and coding research reflects publicly available sources as of the report date and is not billing or coding guidance.

Electronics

FCC and electronics research

For connected and radio-frequency products, the certification landscape shapes the schedule and the bill of materials. We research it so it is understood before it becomes a surprise.

Certification & compliance research

  • Research into which FCC rule parts appear applicable to a product type
  • Certification pathway research, including typical steps and documentation
  • Documented and commonly reported compliance risk areas, drawn from credible published sources
  • Test laboratory and accreditation research
  • Standards landscape research beyond FCC where relevant

Electronics & product research

  • Electronics and connected-product market research
  • Component, module, and technology landscape research
  • Supply base and vendor landscape research
  • Competing product teardown-level feature comparison from public sources
  • Technical documentation support and organization

Same boundary. This is engineering and regulatory research and guidance, not a legal determination of compliance. We do not certify products, issue compliance opinions, or represent clients before the FCC or any other agency. Certification decisions rest with accredited bodies, and legal determinations should come from qualified counsel.

Engineering

Engineering literacy, applied to business questions

Both founders are engineers. That shows up less as a service line and more as a difference in how the research reads.

We can read the primary sources

Datasheets, standards documents, regulatory databases, patents, and published literature are source material for us rather than obstacles to route around.

We know what is hard to build

A competitive claim that looks impressive on a website is sometimes physically or economically implausible. Engineering training is what lets us notice.

We make the visuals too

Product renders, technical illustrations, exploded diagrams, system diagrams, and short explainer animations, produced in-house. The same engagement can deliver the market math and the figures you present it with.
